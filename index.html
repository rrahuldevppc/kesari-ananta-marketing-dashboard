Retention Plan — First Draft
  • Reorder reminders — ghee and laddu are consumables with a natural repurchase cycle (roughly 30-45 days for a household); a timed WhatsApp nudge around that window is the single highest-leverage retention lever available right now.
  • Referral loop — feed CardPing leads captured at the UPITS booth into a referral program: existing buyers get a code, new customers get a discount, referrer gets a reward.
  • Festive gifting reminders — Diwali (~8 Nov 2026) and other festivals are natural repeat-purchase triggers for gifting-sized orders; reminder + gifting-offer combo timed 3-4 weeks ahead.
  • Post-purchase cross-sell — a ghee buyer gets a laddu/dry-fruit cross-sell message after their expected consumption midpoint, and vice versa.
  • Subscription option — a simple "auto-reorder every N days" option for ghee/laddu would convert one-time buyers into recurring revenue without needing new ad spend.
These are starting suggestions based on the product category — confirm or adjust based on what you're actually planning, and this section will track real results against it.
