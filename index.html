The 3 Moves
Move 1
Stack the brand, don't split it
Public sees Kesari Ananta. The agency is the punchline: "we built and run Kesari's whole digital side ourselves — website, WhatsApp automation, CardPing — can do it for you too."
Move 2
Two clocks, one booth
11am–3pm = B2B / corporate / agency money window. 3pm–8pm = public retail / volume window. Same stall, different staffing and script per window.
Move 3
Book conversations, not footfall
Every card gets scanned into CardPing and tagged. The real event happens in the 6 weeks after, working the hot list before Diwali (~8 Nov).
Staff one-liner: "We're Kesari Ananta — pure dry fruits & A2 ghee from Prayagraj. Taste something. And by the way, we're also the team that runs Kesari's whole digital side, so if you sell anything, let's talk."
Critical Risks — fix first
6
5-day event was being planned like a 1–2 day stall. Fix: shift rota + 2 hired retail promoters, not 6 people doing all 5 days.
Mass WhatsApp-blasting 3–4k exhibitors risks a WhatsApp ban + DPDP consent issues. Fix: re-scope to ~120 targeted contacts (60 Kesari-fit, 60 agency-fit), personalized outreach only.
"India's first WhatsApp card scanner" claim for CardPing is false and an ad-law exposure. Never use it. Say instead: "the only one that does capture and automatic follow-up, in Indian languages, priced per event" and lead with "87 cards at one event → ₹12L pipeline."
₹1L "all-in" budget must NOT include sellable retail inventory (~₹40–50k). That's working capital/COGS, recovered from sales — keep OPEX, COGS-stock, and giveaways as 3 separate pots.
Power & WiFi are single points of failure. Need sanctioned kW in writing + dedicated 4G/5G hotspot + spare SIM as backup — expo WiFi is unreliable and will break CardPing/UPI demos.
₹100 giveaway kits, if uncapped, can eat the whole goods margin. Fix: daily cap ~30/day (~150 total), only to qualified prospects.
